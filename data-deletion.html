Data Deletion Instructions

Application: Airbyte Ingest · Last updated: 17 August 2026

What This Application Stores

Airbyte Ingest is a private, internal application operated by Cecareus on behalf of Digital Position, which is the controller of the data it retrieves. It retrieves aggregated advertising performance data — campaigns, ad sets, ads and their associated metrics — from advertising accounts that have explicitly granted access. It does not collect or store personal data belonging to consumers, website visitors or ad recipients, and it has no consumer-facing user accounts.

How to Request Deletion

If you are the owner of an advertising account connected to this application and wish to have all retrieved data deleted, send a request to abustos@valuecastpartners.com including:

  1. The advertising account name and account ID
  2. Confirmation that you are an administrator of that account or its business portfolio

We will delete all data retrieved from that advertising account from our data warehouse and confirm in writing. Requests are actioned within 30 days of receipt.

Revoking Access Directly

You can stop all further data retrieval yourself, immediately and without contacting us, by any of the following:

  • Removing the application's assigned assets in Meta Business Manager (Business Settings → Users → System Users, or Accounts → Ad Accounts)
  • Revoking the access token issued to the application
  • Removing the application from your business portfolio

Revocation takes effect immediately. Any data already retrieved will still be deleted on request as described above.

Contact

All deletion requests and related questions: abustos@valuecastpartners.com.

These instructions apply solely to the Meta application Airbyte Ingest (App ID 2233200627525720). See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
