Privacy Policy

Application: Airbyte Ingest · Last updated: 17 August 2026

Overview

Airbyte Ingest is a private, internal data-integration application built and operated by Cecareus ("we", "us") on behalf of and under the instruction of Digital Position, which determines the purposes and means of the processing and is the controller of the data. It is not offered to the public and has no consumer-facing end users. Its sole function is to read advertising performance data from the Meta Marketing API for advertising accounts that have explicitly granted access, and to load that data into a private data warehouse maintained for Digital Position.

Information We Access

The application reads the following categories of data through the Meta Marketing API:

  • Advertising account metadata (account name, identifier, currency, status)
  • Campaign, ad set, ad and creative configuration
  • Aggregated advertising performance metrics, such as spend, impressions, clicks and conversions

The application performs read-only operations. It does not create, modify, pause or delete campaigns, ad sets, ads or any other object.

Information We Do Not Collect

The application does not collect, request or store personal data belonging to consumers, website visitors or ad recipients. It does not access private messages, contact lists, individual user profiles, payment details or any special-category personal data. All advertising metrics retrieved are aggregated and reported at the campaign, ad set or ad level.

How Data Is Used

Retrieved data is used exclusively for Digital Position's internal advertising performance analysis and reporting. It is not sold, licensed, rented, disclosed to third parties for their own purposes, or used for advertising targeting, profiling or automated decision-making.

Storage and Retention

Data is stored in a private data warehouse hosted on Microsoft Azure, maintained by Cecareus for Digital Position and accessible only to authorised personnel of those two organisations. Access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets and are never exposed to third parties. Data is retained only as long as it is required for internal reporting, and is deleted upon request from the owner of the relevant advertising account.

Data Sharing and Subprocessors

We do not sell, license, rent or otherwise disclose data retrieved through this application to any third party for their own purposes.

Two entities process this data on the controller's behalf: Cecareus, which builds and operates the application and maintains the data warehouse under Digital Position's instruction; and Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft Azure), which provides the cloud infrastructure on which the warehouse is hosted. Both act under contractual obligations of confidentiality and neither uses this data for its own purposes. No other subprocessor receives Meta Platform Data from this application.

Revoking Access

Access is granted through Meta Business Manager and may be revoked at any time by the owner of the advertising account, by removing the application's assigned assets or by revoking the associated access token. Revocation takes effect immediately and stops all further data retrieval.

Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of any data retrieved by this application, see our Data Deletion Instructions, or contact us using the address below. Requests are actioned within 30 days.
