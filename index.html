Privacy Policy

Application: Airbyte Ingest · Last updated: 27 July 2026

Overview

Airbyte Ingest is a private, internal data-integration application operated by Cecareus ("we", "us"). It is not offered to the public and has no end users other than Cecareus. Its sole function is to read advertising performance data from the Meta Marketing API on behalf of advertising accounts that have explicitly granted access, and to load that data into a private data warehouse controlled by Cecareus.

Information We Access

The application reads the following categories of data through the Meta Marketing API:

  • Advertising account metadata (account name, identifier, currency, status)
  • Campaign, ad set, ad and creative configuration
  • Aggregated advertising performance metrics, such as spend, impressions, clicks and conversions

The application performs read-only operations. It does not create, modify, pause or delete campaigns, ad sets, ads or any other object.

Information We Do Not Collect

The application does not collect, request or store personal data belonging to consumers, website visitors or ad recipients. It does not access private messages, contact lists, individual user profiles, payment details or any special-category personal data. All advertising metrics retrieved are aggregated and reported at the campaign, ad set or ad level.

How Data Is Used

Retrieved data is used exclusively for internal advertising performance analysis and reporting. It is not sold, licensed, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising targeting, profiling or automated decision-making.

Storage and Retention

Data is stored in a private data warehouse controlled by Cecareus, accessible only to authorised personnel. Access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets and are never exposed to third parties. Data is retained only as long as it is required for internal reporting, and is deleted upon request from the owner of the relevant advertising account.

Data Sharing

We do not share data retrieved through this application with any third party. No subprocessors receive Meta Platform Data from this application.

Revoking Access

Access is granted through Meta Business Manager and may be revoked at any time by the owner of the advertising account, by removing the application's assigned assets or by revoking the associated access token. Revocation takes effect immediately and stops all further data retrieval.

Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of any data retrieved by this application, contact us using the address below. Requests are actioned within 30 days.
